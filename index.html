Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About ApplyFast AI

1. What is ApplyFast AI and how does it work?
ApplyFast AI is a Chrome extension that helps job applicants auto-fill online application forms faster using resume parsing and saved personal/profile details. You can test the interactive workflow right here on this demo page.
2. Which job boards and application portals are supported?
Pro users get unlimited auto-fills across major hiring platforms including LinkedIn, Greenhouse, Ashby, Workday, and Lever, along with standard web application forms.
3. Is my resume or personal data uploaded to any server?
No. Resume reading and profile details are handled locally in the user’s browser/device without uploading resume files to external databases or selling user data.
4. What is included in the Free tier vs. Pro plans?
New users start with 10 free form auto-fills. Upgrading to Pro unlocks unlimited auto-fills at ₹149 / $2 per month or ₹1,499 / $18 per year (saving ₹289 / $6 annually).
5. How do I receive and activate my Pro license key?
After payment completion, your Pro license key is sent to your email. Paste the key into the "Enter Pro License Key" box and click "Activate Pro" to unlock Pro features.
