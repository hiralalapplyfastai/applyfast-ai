Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About ApplyFast AI

1. What is ApplyFast AI and how does it work?
ApplyFast AI is a Chrome extension that helps job applicants auto-fill online application forms faster using resume parsing and saved personal/profile details. You can test the interactive workflow right here on this demo page.
2. Which job boards and application portals are supported?
Pro users get unlimited auto-fills across major hiring platforms including LinkedIn, Greenhouse, Ashby, Workday, and Lever, along with standard web application forms.
3. Is my resume or personal data uploaded to any server?
No. Resume reading and profile details are handled locally in the user’s browser/device without uploading resume files to external databasesor selling user data.
4. What is included in the Free tier vs. Pro plans?
New users start with 10 free form auto-fills. Upgrading to Pro unlocks unlimited auto-fills at ₹149 / $2 per month or ₹1,499 / $18 per year (saving ₹289 / $6 annually).
5. How do I receive and activate my Pro license key?
After payment completion, your Pro license key is sent to your email. Paste the key into the "Enter Pro License Key" box and click "Activate Pro" to unlock Pro features.
6. What is the refund and cancellation policy?
Because ApplyFast AI provides 10 free forms upfront and instant digital license delivery, all Pro purchases are final and non-refundable. Auto-renewals can be cancelled before the next billing date.
7. What file formats are supported for resume parsing?
ApplyFast AI supports PDF and DOCX resume formats so applicants can extract details quickly and review or edit fields before auto-filling a job application.
8. Can I overwrite fields that already contain text?
Yes. You can check "Replace fields that already have values" if you want existing form values replaced, or leave it unchecked to fill only empty fields safely.
9. How does the Yearly Pro plan save money?
Paying ₹1,499 / $18 once per year gives full-year Pro access and saves ₹289 / $6 compared to paying ₹149 / $2 every month for 12 months.
10. Is there any physical shipping or hardware involved?
No. ApplyFast AI is a 100% digital browser extension and software license product; delivery is electronic via browser extension and email license key.
11. Why is an interactive web demo provided here?
This live web page lets users and reviewers test the ApplyFast AI form auto-fill flow, plan options, FAQs, and legal policies directly in a browser without needing prior installation.
12. How can I contact support or check updates?
You can check project updates, documentation, and support details on the official site at hiralalapplyfastai.github.io/applyfast-ai or via the GitHub repository link below.
