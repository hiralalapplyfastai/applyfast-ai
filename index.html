How it works

Three steps, no retyping

Save your profile

Upload your resume (PDF or DOCX) and the extension reads it on your device to fill your profile. You can also type or edit any field yourself.

Open any application

Go to the job application form on any website. The extension looks at each field's label and name to work out what it is asking for.

Click Auto-fill

One click fills the matching empty fields, including Yes/No questions like work authorization. You review everything and submit it yourself.

FAQ

Questions and answers

How do I install ApplyFast AI?
  1. Download the extension package from the GitHub repository (Code, then Download ZIP) and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the extension folder.
  4. Pin ApplyFast AI to your toolbar, open it, and save your profile or upload your resume.
Where is my data stored? Is it safe?

Your profile is stored in your own Chrome storage (which Chrome may sync across your signed-in devices). Resumes are read on your device and are never uploaded. ApplyFast AI has no servers that receive your profile. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Does it write answers with AI?

No. It fills the text you saved, such as your professional summary and cover letter. Field matching is rule-based and runs locally in your browser, so your data is not sent to an AI service.

Which sites and fields work?

Standard text boxes, text areas, dropdowns and Yes/No radio buttons on most job sites. Search-style custom dropdowns and file upload buttons (such as resume attachments) cannot be filled automatically, so you handle those yourself.

What counts as one free auto-fill?

One click on Auto-fill that fills at least one field. If nothing matches on a page, no free use is deducted. You get 10 in total.

How do billing and license keys work?

Choose Monthly or Yearly and pay through the Dodo Payments checkout. Your license key is emailed automatically after payment. Paste it into the extension and click Activate Pro. Plans renew automatically until you cancel the renewal. All purchases are final and non-refundable (see the Refund Policy).
