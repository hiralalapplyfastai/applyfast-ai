FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I install the ApplyFast AI Chrome extension?
  1. Download the extension package from our GitHub repository and unzip the folder.
  2. Open chrome://extensions in Google Chrome and enable Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and select the extracted extension folder.
  4. Pin ApplyFast AI to your toolbar, save your profile or upload your resume, and start auto-filling applications.
How do billing and Pro license keys work?

Click Choose Monthly or Choose Yearly and complete payment on the secure Dodo Payments checkout page. Your license key is sent to your email automatically after payment. Paste the key into the ApplyFast AI extension and click Activate Pro.

Where is my personal profile data stored?

Your profile is saved locally inside your own Chrome browser storage. Resumes are parsed locally on your device and are never uploaded to external servers.

Which websites and form fields are supported?

Standard text inputs, text areas, dropdowns, and Yes/No radio buttons across major job boards and career sites. File attachment buttons (like uploading a resume PDF) must still be selected manually due to browser security rules.
